# Elite Community Contract v1

## Shared across every sport
- Elite friends
- direct messages
- notifications
- blocks
- reports
- clickable Elite usernames
- public profile routing

## Sport-specific
Each sport may also maintain its own league/team/community surfaces. Those communities can use Elite identity without forcing every sport into the same presentation.

## Safety and moderation
- blocking immediately prevents new friend/DM interactions
- reporting is a Core responsibility
- message length is bounded
- production deployment must add rate limits, spam controls, moderation queues, and retention policies

## Identity rule
Sport athlete names may differ. Social actions attach to the Elite account, not merely the athlete display name.

Example:
`Thomas Garner` may be the Baseball player.
`T. Garner` may be the Racing driver.
Both resolve to the same Elite public profile.

# Elite Sports Design Doctrine

## Shared identity. Separate worlds. Connected legacy.

1. The Hub unifies people, not gameplay.
2. Every sport controls its own visual identity, terminology, navigation, progression, presentation, simulation, statistics, culture, and competitive rhythm.
3. Elite Core owns the person: one permanent `elite_user_id`.
4. Each sport owns the athlete: sport-specific name, appearance, build, team, record, and career.
5. Cross-sport Legacy records history and recognition; it never grants competitive attribute advantages.
6. Careers never disappear. Retired careers, championships, awards, records, and Hall of Fame history remain in the Career Passport.
7. Sports are independently deployable.
8. Core consumes small standardized summaries rather than sport databases.
9. Historical integrity matters: old seasons and accomplishments remain browsable.
10. Elite begins with a clean slate. Legends, dynasties, rivalries, and records are created by the community.
